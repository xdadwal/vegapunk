[.github/CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS) makes the maintainer the reviewer for every file. How decisions are made is in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## What maintainers do

Maintainers review and merge pull requests, triage issues, answer in Discussions, keep the scope and conventions in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) up to date, handle security reports ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and conduct reports ([CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)), and make releases ([docs/releasing.md](docs/releasing.md)).

Vegapunk is maintained in spare time. All of this is **best effort**, with no guaranteed response time.

## Becoming a maintainer

There is no application. People who have made **sustained, good-quality contributions** over a while, and who help others through reviews, triage and answers, may be invited by the existing maintainers. A new maintainer is added to this file and to CODEOWNERS in a pull request.

## Stepping down

A maintainer can step down at any time, for any reason, by saying so or opening a pull request against this file. Former maintainers are listed below as emeritus, with thanks. A maintainer who has been inactive for a long time may be asked whether they want to move to emeritus.
